# Discovering profiles to track

Resolve seed relationships using current source evidence, then test their audience.
Use the following routes when the user has not supplied a source list.

| Category | Where to start | What establishes the relationship | What to inspect next |
|---|---|---|---|
| Competitor | Supplied competitors; offer/product comparison; verified adjacent alternatives | Relevant competing offering, then the person's current role at that company | Founders, commercial leaders, subject experts and active personnel publishing to the intended buyers |
| Influencer | Practitioner publications, conference speakers, relevant community contributors | Attributable public work addressing the buyer's domain | Original posts and actual audience, not follower count alone |
| Partner | Supplied partners; official integration or channel directory | A documented current relationship relevant to this offer or its buyers | Active partner personnel explaining joint customer work |
| Customer | Supplied customers; official case studies or public customer stories | A customer relationship actually supported by the source; historic stories labelled historic | Public advocates or subject experts and relevant posts |
| Recruiter | Specialist firms or people publishing into the buyer's professional community | Demonstrated relevant recruiting practice; never treat all recruiter audiences as buyers | Audience contamination by candidates/recruiters versus target-role participants |
| Owned | User-selected connected account | Host-confirmed ownership/access and allowed scope | Authored posts through the authorized interface |

Record `relationshipTo` (offer/company/community), not just a label. A security
vendor is not automatically a competitor of every other security vendor. A
partner of a competitor is not automatically the user's partner. A target-company
employee is not automatically the user's customer. If the relation is unresolved,
mark the relationship unsupported and research it rather than report it verified.
Keep a verified manual identity active with the supplied category label, while
separately disclosing unsupported or contradicted relationship claims. Research-
selected sources remain proposed until the selection relationship is supported.

Useful search shapes are hypotheses, not executable provider requirements:
company domain + team/leadership; exact company + role + LinkedIn; official
customer story + named spokesperson; partner directory + company personnel;
conference subject + speaker. Use public primary sources or authorized connected
data and preserve the actual returned URL. Search snippets alone can suggest a
seed, but need corroboration for a material relationship/current-role claim.

For each resolved seed inspect recent original activity before buying deep
audience coverage. Prioritize a mix of categories and authors where budget permits.
Keep manual configuration supported even when discovery finds nothing. No useful
publishing, insufficient identity evidence and inaccessible activity are different
outcomes. Do not replace an inaccessible seed with a same-name person.

Unsupported means the relationship is unproven; contradicted requires affirmative
evidence incompatible with that specific claim. Being a competitor's customer
alone does not disprove being the user's partner.
